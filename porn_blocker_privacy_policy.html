Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Porn Blocker ("the App") handles information when you use the Android application. The App is designed to help users reduce access to adult content and manage personal blocking and commitment settings.

1. Information We Handle

Account information: If you sign in with Google or email/password, Firebase Authentication processes the information needed to create and authenticate your account. Depending on the sign-in method, this may include your email address, a user identifier, and basic profile information made available by the identity provider.

On-device app information: Your commitment settings, selected blocking preferences, and website blocklist are stored locally on your device, as configured in the current version of the App. This information is used to provide the App's features.

Browsing information used for blocking: If you enable the App's Accessibility Service, Android provides the App with limited information about the currently displayed app or page so it can identify content or website addresses for blocking. This processing is used only to operate the blocking feature. The App does not intentionally collect or transmit your browsing history, visited page contents, or detected website addresses to our servers.

2. Accessibility Service

The App uses Android's Accessibility Service only to support its content-blocking functionality. When enabled, the service may inspect relevant on-screen text, app identifiers, or browser page information needed to determine whether content should be blocked. This information is processed on your device for the blocking decision and is not used to build an advertising profile, track you across apps, or sell your activity data.

You can turn off the Accessibility Service at any time in Android Settings. Blocking features that depend on it may stop working when it is disabled.

3. How We Use Information

  • To detect and block content or websites according to your settings.
  • To maintain the security and basic operation of the App.
  • To respond to support requests that you send to us.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Account authentication is provided by Google Firebase Authentication. Your local blocking preferences, commitment settings, and blocklist are stored on your device and are not uploaded by the App as part of its normal operation.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your browsing activity or blocklist with advertisers. Firebase may process authentication information as a service provider under its own terms and privacy documentation.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.
